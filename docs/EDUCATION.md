# AROMOTION Education Access

AROMOTION should remain affordable for students and teachers.

## Student Edition

**Price: FREE**

Students verified with an approved academic email domain receive **AROMOTION Student Edition** at no charge.

The first approved academic domains are:

- `@my.uopeople.edu` — University of the People student accounts
- `@studmc.kiu.ac.ug` — Kampala International University student accounts

Individual student email addresses are deliberately **not committed to this public repository**. Verification uses the academic domain so that personal addresses do not have to be published in source code.

## Planned Student Edition features

The Student Edition is intended to include the complete core tutorial workflow:

- Lossless/high-quality screen recording
- Automatic zoom
- Smooth cursor motion
- Click effects
- Shortcut display
- 3D motion
- Annotations
- Microphone and system audio
- Webcam overlay
- Local captions when available
- 1080p/60 export
- No watermark

Commercial/team/cloud features may later be separated from Student Edition, but the core teaching and learning workflow should remain usable without a subscription.

## Adding another institution

A new academic domain can be reviewed and added to `EducationAccessService.ApprovedAcademicDomains` without publishing any student's full email address.
